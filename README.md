**Cravora** is an independent, premium food-tech web application that combines the visual sophistication of a luxury culinary publication with the speed and convenience of modern online ordering. Tailored exclusively for **Nagpur, Maharashtra**, Cravora showcases authentic Saoji kitchens, rustic highway dhabas, 5-star hotel dining, youth startup resto-cafes, and 100% Pure Veg heritage institutions.

🌐 **Live Website**: [https://cravora-gilt.vercel.app](https://cravora-gilt.vercel.app)

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **🍽️ Interactive Multi-Hotel Dish Slideshow**:
  - Auto-advancing culinary slideshow on the hero banner with pause-on-hover.
  - Features signature dishes from Radisson Blu, The Royal Saoji, Vishnuji Ki Rasoi, Sardarji Da Dhaba, Plan B Resto-Cafe, Haldiram's Heritage, and more.
  - Interactive hotel selector pills, timer progress indicator, and direct **"Add to Cart"** functionality from the slide.

- **📍 100% Nagpur-Centric Dining Ecosystem**:
  - Live location selector across 12 authentic Nagpur zones: Dharampeth & WHC Road, Sadar & Civil Lines, Ramdaspeth, Wardha Road, Amravati Road Highway (Wadi), VNIT / Bajaj Nagar, Futala Lake, Mominpura, etc.
  - Curated categories: *Saoji & Vidarbha*, *Highway Dhabas*, *5-Star Luxury Hotels*, *Youth Startup Cafes*, *Nagpur Sweets & Mithai*, and *100% Pure Veg*.

- **🌱 Dedicated Pure Veg Filter & Institutions**:
  - Strict **"Pure Veg"** filter isolating 6 authentic 100% pure vegetarian institutions: *Vishnuji Ki Rasoi*, *Naivedhyam*, *Shree Shivkrupa Pure Veg Dhaba*, *Veeraswami Dakshin*, *The Daily Roast Bistro*, and *Haldiram's Heritage*.

- **🔍 Multi-Facet Restaurant Exploration**:
  - Filter by cuisine, rating (4.5+), price tiers (₹, ₹₹, ₹₹₹), delivery time (< 30 min), and active discounts.
  - Real-time search with instant auto-suggestions, search history, and cuisine tags.

- **🛒 Full Cart & Financial Calculation**:
  - Live basket calculation: subtotal, dynamic delivery fees, GST breakdown, and platform fee.
  - Multi-restaurant conflict detection modal with item replacement confirmation.
  - Working coupon codes (`CRAVORA50`, `GOURMET100`, `FIRSTBITE`, `FEAST20`).
  - Persistent cart state across page reloads using `localStorage`.

- **💳 Simulated Checkout & Live Order Tracking**:
  - Address selection with customizable delivery instructions (e.g. *"Ring doorbell"*, *"Leave at door"*).
  - Multiple payment options: UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Card, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery.
  - Live animated order status timeline with real-time progress steps and celebration confetti.
